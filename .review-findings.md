# Review findings

- Desktop preview renders the dark operations-console theme correctly after aligning `ThemeProvider` with the dark design tokens.
- Mobile preview at 375px keeps the header actions and primary controls readable; the timeline intentionally scrolls horizontally within its card.
- Focus-visible outlines were added globally for keyboard navigation.
- Reduced-motion users now receive near-instant transitions and disabled animation.
- The remaining visual tradeoff is the intentionally dense timeline, which preserves its horizontal planning grid on small screens rather than collapsing data into unreadable cards.
